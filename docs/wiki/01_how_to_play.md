# How to play

## Getting started
1. In the main menu choose **New game** → pick a country on the map or from the list → **Play**.
2. The game starts **paused** (1 January 1936). Space starts/pauses, 1–5 set the speed.
3. The game starts in English; switch to Turkish in **Settings → Language** (main menu or Esc menu).

## The first 30 days (any country)
1. **Pick a state program (F)**: bright programs can be chosen. A program takes 4–12 weeks; its effect applies when it ends.
2. **Fill the research slots (I)**: an empty slot glows red. A technology ahead of its year takes +150% time per year early.
3. **Construction (T)**: civilian factories grow the economy, military factories grow output. Pick a building → click a
   state on the map, or click a state and use "Build here".
4. **Production (Y)**: assign idle military factories to production lines. An idle factory produces nothing.
5. **Trade (R)**: a resource shortfall is a red row. **Buy** → pick the selling country. Automatic trade runs only if you
   switch it on.
6. **Politics (Q)**: once you have 150 influence, change a law or hire an advisor.
7. **Army (U)**: group your divisions into armies, give each a general and a front (see [Warfare](05_warfare.md)).

## Controls
| Action | Shortcut |
|---|---|
| Pause / resume | Space |
| Speed 1–5, faster/slower | 1–5, + / − |
| Map modes: political, terrain, states, routes | F1 F2 F3 F4 |
| Back to the capital | Home |
| Quick save | F5 |
| Menu / close panels | Esc |
| Politics, Program, Research, Diplomacy | Q, F, I, O |
| Trade, Construction, Production, Army | R, T, Y, U |
| Navy, Air, Logistics | N, H, L |
| Select a division / box select / add to selection | Left click / drag / Shift |
| Move or attack order | Right click (on the web and on Mac: Ctrl + left click while divisions are selected) |
| A country's overall situation | Hold Ctrl over the map |
| That country's politics screen (read-only) | Ctrl + left click (no divisions selected) |
| Camera | Middle-button drag, wheel, two fingers |

Movement arrows are drawn **green** (move) or **red** (attack into enemy land). At far zoom small country flags replace
division counts; further out unit markers are hidden to keep the map clean.

## Winning and losing
- **Losing**: your country surrenders. Surrender progress is the share of your cities' victory points held by the enemy
  (colonies count ¼, +10% if the capital falls); the limit is 80%, dropping as low as 50% when the home front is under 50%
  (see [Diplomacy](06_diplomacy.md)).
- **Winning**: stay standing until 1 January 1948. The end screen appears on that date. To conquer the world, repeat the
  chain casus belli → war → surrender. The occupied states of a surrendering country pass to the occupier.

## Tips for Turkey
- In May 1936 the **Soviet pressure over the Straits** arrives: a polite refusal recalling Atatürk's friendship (only while
  Atatürk leads), granting bases, or refusal + mobilisation (gives the Soviets a casus belli).
- **November 1938**: Atatürk dies; you choose the successor (İnönü, Çakmak, Bayar).
- **October 1939**: the Moscow talks: a treaty with Britain and France, the Soviet demands, or mobilisation.
- Turkey is non-aligned: preparing a casus belli needs a crisis index of 50%. Without imported steel the factories cannot
  run at full output.
