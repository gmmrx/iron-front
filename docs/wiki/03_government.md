# Government

## Ideology and ruling party
Every country has an ideology (democratic, communist, fascist, non-aligned), a leader and a ruling party. Ideology
popularity is shown in the pie on the Politics screen. **The popularity of the ruling ideology adds to stability: +15% at 100%.**

**Elections:** in countries with elections (USA every 4 years, Britain 1939, etc.) power changes hands if another ideology
passes 50% on election day. Elections are postponed during war. The player is told the result through an event.

## Stability
Effective stability = base + contributions of national conditions/laws/advisors + ruling party contribution (0–100%).

| Stability | Effect |
|---|---|
| 100% | Factory output +20%, influence +10%, consumer goods −20% |
| 50% | Neutral |
| Below 50% | Construction speed penalty (up to −25%) |
| 0% | Factory output −50%, influence −20% |

## Home front
The people's will to keep fighting. Effective home front = base + contributions + **crisis index** (+0.4% for every 1%,
at most +40%) + **war situation** (+20% in a defensive war, −20% in a war of aggression).

- The stronger conscription laws need a strong home front.
- The **surrender limit** is 80%; when the home front is below 50% it drops by 0.6 × the difference (lowest 50%, lower
  still with national conditions).

## Influence
The government's power to act at home and abroad. Daily **+2 × (1 + bonuses + stability effect)** + flat bonuses.
Spent on: changing a law **150**, an advisor **150**, decisions, a casus belli **30**.

## Laws
Changing a law costs 150 influence; a law whose requirement is not met is locked (the reason is in the tooltip).

| Law | Effect | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Treaty-Limited Army | Recruitable population 0.7% | — |
| Professional Army | Recruitable population 1.4% | — |
| One-Year Service | Recruitable population 2.6% | — |

| Law | Effect | Requirement |

## National conditions
Country-specific, permanent or timed effects drawn from historical facts (e.g. Turkey: Kemalist Officers, Resistance to
the Reforms, Army in Reorganization, Low Literacy [19% in the 1935 census], First Five-Year Industrial Plan [ends
17 April 1938]). Some state programs remove negative conditions (e.g. Kemalist State → Resistance to the Reforms; Village
Institutes → Low Literacy; Army Modernization → Army in Reorganization).

## Other countries' politics
Hold **Ctrl** over a country on the map for its overall situation; **Ctrl + click** opens its Politics screen read-only
(leader, parties, laws, advisors, decisions in force, current state program). "Back to our government" returns to yours.
